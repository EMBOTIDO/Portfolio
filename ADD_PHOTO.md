# How to Add Your Profile Picture

To add your profile picture to the portfolio:

1. **Place your photo** in the `public` folder
2. **Name it** `profile.jpg` (or update the path in `src/App.js`)
3. **Recommended size**: 500x500 pixels or larger (square images work best)
4. **Format**: JPG, PNG, or WebP

The image will automatically appear in the hero section with a nice circular border and glow effect!

If the image doesn't load, a placeholder will be shown instead.
